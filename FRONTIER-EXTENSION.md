# Slate: develop knowledge the company does not yet have

**Requirements and current scope.** Slate v0.1.0 defaults to the core-wiki profile. Federation, granted actions and sustained inquiry remain explicit flagship development functionality with full acceptance pending. [ACCEPTANCE.md](./ACCEPTANCE.md) governs all 220 current product clauses, 22 baseline obligations, eight policy profiles and six journeys. The requirements below remain binding; they do not establish provider, prospective-value or public-release acceptance. [HANDOVER.md](./HANDOVER.md) records current source behavior and verification limits.

Slate should help a company discover what matters, work out what to do, test it and get better at doing it again. A dependable answer to an existing policy question is valuable. The larger opportunity is to develop an explanation or method that nobody in the company already has, then turn the expensive discovery into inexpensive, checked recurring work.

Sustained inquiry requires agents to pursue difficult questions over bounded, durable stages, generate rival hypotheses, seek disconfirmation, run reproducible analyses and derive useful conclusions. These are product requirements whose quality must be measured on real permitted work. A checker can establish a bounded logical property; agreement between models cannot establish how customers behave.

## Six extensions that change the work

### 1. Open questions become research programs

A team can keep “Why is time to customer value rising?” open until it has a useful explanation and tested response. From the request and available work, Slate prepares the decision context, competing explanations, discriminating observations, unresolved dependencies and next useful investigation. Agents pursue independent lines in parallel and propose new mechanisms when existing explanations fail. A program can span weeks with durable progress rather than an indefinitely running request. Its output can be a discovery absent from every source, with a reproducible argument connecting observations to that conclusion. Members can inspect or correct the inquiry; they do not author research schemas or coordinate agents.

### 2. Ambient preparation brings consequential questions forward

As permitted events arrive, inexpensive semantic checks identify relevance, novelty, possible contradiction and likely impact on active objectives. Slate keeps relevant context ready and proposes a question when a pattern merits investigation, including when no existing claim depends on it. The member sees why it surfaced and a prepared next step, not a queue of events to curate. Direct frontier sweeps and sampled investigations of low-ranked events protect against a fast judgment hiding an important anomaly.

### 3. Reasoning becomes a reusable, checked asset

Slate retains explicit assumptions, domain models, applicable populations, counterexamples, failed hypotheses, executable calculations and reproducible analyses as concise explanatory artifacts, not private reasoning transcripts. A later investigator can replay a method, inspect what changed, and reuse only the applicable portion. Keep observations, assumptions, deductions and business choices distinct. A deduction is conditional on its inputs; a citation alone does not validate an inference. Where a mathematical or logical property matters, retain the solver/checker version, bounded proposition, input and result so another run can check it independently.

### 4. Policies and plans can be reasoned about executably

Slate translates a bounded operational choice into explicit objectives and constraints—for example, which eligible accounts could receive an intervention within contact restrictions and staff capacity. It derives feasible allocations, contradictions, sensitivity and counterexamples, beyond a before/after policy rehearsal. Code checks exact arithmetic and invariants; the model proposes and explains the formulation. Keep the accepting owner and assumptions visible. A feasible allocation does not prove that an intervention improves retention. Slate needs useful local models, not a universal simulator, and members do not design the methodology.

### 5. Outcomes improve the company's methods

Slate records forecasts before a decision and compares them with exposure, outcomes and effect receipts. Unchanged source text can still contain a theory that reality disproves. It checks selection bias, changed circumstances and measurement quality before crediting an intervention, and retains null and adverse findings alongside successes. A learned method, prompt, criterion or test is revisioned and evaluated before activation; this does not automatically tune model weights or share customer data across deployments. A discovery can become tested, owner-controlled software or a skill through ordinary authoring. Repeated checks can then use verified code and targeted judgments instead of repeating the same deep investigation.

### 6. Inquiry, experiment and task interfaces support collaboration

Slate prepares question and experiment views from the work already underway. On demand they can show rival explanations, evidence that would change the conclusion, calculations, comparison, observation window, exposure, guardrails and next test. A task view presents the actual decision and authorized effects; outcomes return to the same records. These contextual views preserve the editor, comments, tree, history and accepted Slate UI for collaboration. A task, connected context or external agent can be the entry point. Members do not write an experiment plan or turn results into learning by hand.

## Fast preparation, deep investigation, deterministic control

Slate routes work across complementary roles by measured task fit within owner policy; members do not need a model selector. Fast models make plentiful **typed semantic decisions**: relevant to which question, apparently new, likely consequential, worth ranking higher, or possibly inconsistent. Frontier models generate explanations, direct investigations, compose analyses and challenge conclusions. Deterministic code enforces ACLs, source authority records, effect admission, budgets and exact calculations. A routing score cannot supply consent or permission.

No particular fast-model vendor is required. Evaluate candidate classifiers and routing models on the actual permitted task distribution, including arithmetic, multi-hop evidence, irrelevant context and adversarial inputs. A score or probability is not evidence of truth. Record quality, missed events, latency and total cost before expanding a model's role; provider marketing claims do not establish Slate performance.

Route by declared work requirements. An uncertain event, consequential question or reasoning-intensive task can explicitly request frontier investigation. Owner questions and scheduled independent frontier sweeps bypass fast ranking, and low-score audits measure missed-important-event recall. This is designed work routing, not a silent model-failure fallback. Invalid output still fails visibly under the existing lifecycle; no substitute answer is fabricated.

Schedule around questions and expected information gain, not one research run per event. Deduplicate equivalent work within an authorized audience, combine overlapping observations, and reuse revision-pinned context only after checking source freshness, applicability and the intended audience. A changed assumption, source revision or permission invalidates dependent context before reuse. Keep compute ceilings, durable cost reservations and an attention budget for interruptions; no model raises its own allowance.

While deep work continues, retain what has been learned, which explanation failed, what remains uncertain, the next informative step and cost. A member can inspect progress, narrow scope, stop future work or revoke a grant; independent authorized branches may continue when one is blocked. Stop a branch when it cannot add useful information, its budget ends, or evidence is inaccessible, and record why and what would restart it. Ask the accountable owner only for a missing business choice or authority. Today surfaces meaningful outcomes and decisions, not every successful run or maintenance check. Long programs use bounded stages and durable waits, never an unlimited Running state.

## A future journey: improve time to customer value

**Illustrative future behavior, not current customer evidence or implemented functionality.** A product leader asks: “Why is time to customer value rising, and what change reduces it without increasing support work?” Fast signals from permitted product events suggest that recently activated teams take longer to complete their first useful shared task. Slate links an inquiry to Orbit funnel records, permitted HappyToHelp conversations and product/release evidence. Source access determines what each investigator and output audience can see.

Parallel investigators examine onboarding friction, team handoff, cohort mix, poor product fit and changed usage timing. One checks the instrumentation against release history and actual event definitions; another analyzes comparable cohorts; another studies support evidence; another prepares intervention options. The instrumentation investigator discovers that a release moved the “first value” event later in the workflow. That falsifies the initial claim that the whole apparent deterioration is real. Slate corrects the comparison and records the counterexample rather than preserving an attractive initial story.

The corrected view still suggests a delay within comparable multi-person teams. Cohort analysis tests whether a changed customer mix explains it; support evidence tests whether setup friction dominates; process evidence tests a missing handoff between the installer and teammates. Suppose the remaining evidence supports the handoff explanation more strongly. It remains an empirical hypothesis, not causal proof. A useful interim conclusion is already available: fix the measurement definition and avoid an unsupported blanket onboarding rewrite. The team need not wait for every unknown to settle.

Slate develops an explicit handoff model and computes a bounded allocation for a trial: eligible teams, available staff time, permitted contact frequency and a support-load ceiling. Code checks exact quantities; a solver may verify feasibility under stated constraints. The workspace compares alternative allocations and sensitivity to uncertain effort estimates. It does not label an allocation's mathematical validity as proof of customer benefit.

The accountable owner accepts the bounded trial or an existing grant covers the exact proposed effect. Before execution, record eligibility, treatment and comparison assignment, baseline definition, measurement window, expected effect, smallest meaningful effect, precision needed to distinguish it from no effect, stopping conditions and guardrails. Prefer a defensible randomized comparison where feasible; disclose remaining confounding otherwise. Owning products execute only authorized changes: Orbit retains CRM and outreach; HappyToHelp retains support conversations and replies; the product owns its onboarding behavior. Slate records exact receipts and observed exposure, distinguishes attempted from confirmed changes, and tracks missing observations.

At the declared window, compare time to value and support work with the predeclared comparison. A benefit supports a scoped next step, with uncertainty and applicability attached. A null result counts against the proposed mechanism only if the test is sufficiently precise to rule out the predeclared meaningful effect. Otherwise it remains inconclusive, with the next information needed recorded. An adverse support-load result stops further trial effects under the agreed guardrail and triggers authorized recovery. Incomplete exposure or changed instrumentation can also make the result inconclusive. Every branch revises the explanation and investigative method, preserving the original forecast and decision. Slate owns the inquiry and accumulated knowledge; it has not absorbed the other products.

## First build and proof of value

Build **one complete question → investigation → bounded experiment → outcome → reusable learning loop**, using the customer-activation question family and real permitted sources. It depends on the prior memory, access, revision, collaboration and run foundations. Keep the three minimum-release workflows—SSO inquiry/action/article repair, policy impact/rollout, and research-to-decision/onboarding—and the full flagship and all 220 current product clauses and 22 baseline obligations. This loop adds an acceptance outcome; a research chat demo does not replace them. Broader ambient discovery and richer domain models follow evidence from repeated complete loops.

Compare identical questions against both an expert with current tools and a capable generic agent given the same accessible sources and tools. Use historically held-out cases and prospective investigations with predeclared windows. Historical cases must expose only information available at the decision cutoff: subsequent outcomes, later summaries, derived labels and retrieval indexes must not leak the answer into investigators' context. Freeze evaluation cases separately from development examples; prospective results test whether retrospective advantages survive.

Measure missed-important-event recall, falsified hypotheses, useful new discoveries, reproducibility, forecast calibration, intervention selection and observed outcomes. Include all attempts, model/tool expense, elapsed time, owner attention, interruptions, failures and later repair. Repeated work must demonstrate savings without reusing invalid assumptions. Shadow-compare fast routing with direct frontier investigation over the same permitted event stream; audit low-ranked events and measure total pipeline economics, not just classifier speed.

Continue expansion only when repeated cases preserve the agreed quality floor and improve useful outcomes or total cost/time/attention against those baselines. Stop or revise a method when its discoveries fail replication, important events are missed, or savings disappear after repairs. Privacy and authority failures cannot be traded for productivity. All prior source-publication, consent, credential, exact-receipt and run-lifecycle requirements remain intact.
