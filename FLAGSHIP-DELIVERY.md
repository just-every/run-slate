# Slate flagship: contract and delivery plan

**Requirements and current scope.** Slate v0.1.0 defaults to the core-wiki profile. Federation, granted actions and sustained inquiry remain explicit flagship development functionality with full acceptance pending. [ACCEPTANCE.md](./ACCEPTANCE.md) governs all 220 current product clauses, 22 baseline obligations, eight policy profiles and six journeys. The requirements below remain binding; they do not establish provider, prospective-value or public-release acceptance. [HANDOVER.md](./HANDOVER.md) records current source behavior and verification limits.

## Product record and authority model

Use durable, ordinary domain records and explicit links. The existing page, structured-record, revision, access, decision and operation foundations remain useful. The flagship adds the minimum records required to preserve lineage across systems:

| Record | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| **Connected source** | Provider and tenant identity, installed scopes, account/principal mapping, supported read/write effects, sync cursor, last success/error, declared freshness window, known lag, permission freshness, deletion/revocation support, and source owner. A connector must say when a needed guarantee is unsupported. |
| **Source revision and passage** | Immutable source-native ID, revision/content hash, observed and source times, retained original or restorable reference as permitted, extraction version, and stable passage anchors. Links resolve to the cited revision or a content-free tombstone. Imported and connected sources use their own provenance. |
| **Claim** | One reviewable proposition with exact passage links, claim kind, account/product/plan/audience scope, jurisdiction or other applicability where relevant, effective and expiry interval, owner, verification state, and conflict/unknown status. Extraction confidence and popularity are evidence hints only. |
| **Authority and decision** | The named person or delegated office allowed to approve a subject and scope; decision rationale, evidence revisions, applicability, effective date, approval actor, and immutable accepted revision. New decisions explicitly supersede older ones without erasing them. A source's recency or AI confidence never grants authority. |
| **Live operational observation** | A typed response from the system that owns the current account or process state, including source, observation time, state, and freshness. A document can explain what should happen; it cannot stand in for live entitlement or activation. Snapshots remain snapshots. |
| **Procedure/skill and run** | A versioned procedure with declared context, preconditions, allowed tools, read/write effect grants, budgets, expected outputs, checks and evaluations. A durable run records its exact skill/model/provider version, inputs, decisions, status, pause/resume points, effects, receipts, and uncertainty reconciliation. |

The product should show these links in ordinary page and task views; a graph database is not a prerequisite. Keep access at the record and source boundaries, with server-side checks and reader-specific retrieval. Preserve exact revision citations and accepted decision immutability.

People ask for an outcome from a page, assigned task, or connected work. Slate gathers evidence, prepares its analysis and views, runs independent investigations, and tracks follow-through; users do not create schemas, curate claims, select models, author skills, design research methods, or coordinate agents. Reuse existing authorized instructions and standing grants without repeated approval, compiling each run's scope into bounded internal capabilities. When a source permission or provider credential is absent, ask its named owner for the smallest useful grant. When an actual business decision or effect authority is missing, ask one concise question with a recommended default, alternatives, uncertainty, and consequences. Count setup and colleague/admin work in the measured cost instead of hiding it. Keep material uncertainty, paid effects, audience, and unfinished state in the result; show sources, cost, impact, and run controls on demand. Members can inspect, narrow scope, stop future work, or revoke a grant; stopping cancels unsent work and keeps confirmed receipts. Any reversal requires provider support and a separate valid grant.

## Federation, freshness and revocation

Treat every connector as an explicit contract, not as a pipe. It declares identity, scopes, which principals it can represent, whether it preserves source ACLs or uses a named shared service identity, discoverability rules, sync and webhook behavior, retention, deletion, revocation semantics, and effect support. Show coverage and last successful sync beside results. Index incompleteness, lag, unavailable providers, permission uncertainty, and unsupported effects stay visible.

Recheck authorization at retrieval, answer generation, every long-run resume, and each external effect. When access is revoked, remove that source's passages from future retrieval and derived caches, invalidate affected stored proposals and answers, and prevent those artifacts from disclosing retained quotes. If an upstream connector cannot establish that a reader may still see the content, fail closed for that source and explain the unavailable status. Deletion creates appropriate content-free tombstones where the owning contract requires history, and removes the deleted material from indexes, embeddings, proposals and exports. Never silently repoint a citation to a newer revision.

Interactive work is bounded by the current asking member's access, even when investigators or connectors use broader service credentials. Effective read is the intersection of that member's current source ACL, owner policy, task scope and grants. Background work declares an authorized output audience and destination before it starts; every contributing source must authorize that audience as well as the executing identity. A service identity supplies technical capability, never disclosure authority. Recheck all contributing sources before fan-in, generation, writing, delivering, and each later read of a persisted answer, shared task view or external-agent output. Check source, requester, audience and destination changes again before resume or use; invalidate or withhold derived artifacts when access cannot be established. Restricted facts must not leak through summaries, hints, titles, counts or citations. Do not copy private material into a public page by default: explicit publication and destination permission remain necessary, and a write grant cannot override source audience restrictions, `LIFE-002` or `AI-013`.

Connector and external-agent credentials have a named issuer/owner, represented identity, explicit scopes and destinations, expiry and revocation record. Issue or widen them only under the corresponding owner's authority. Revoke/disconnect immediately blocks new dispatch and propagates to queued work, active steps, caches and derived-output access; show the connection as unavailable. Suspend queued/paused work and stop in-flight work at the next safe boundary, cancelling unsent effects. Reconcile an already dispatched effect without inventing success or retrying under revoked authority; if authorized read-back is unavailable, retain an unresolved receipt until authorized reconciliation is possible. Reconnection is a new authorization check, not restoration of old rights: verify identity and scopes, resynchronize source ACLs/deletions/revisions, invalidate stale retained material, and reauthorize each affected run and output audience before resuming. A changed identity or narrower scope never inherits the old credential's authority.

On a source change, record the observed revision and diff; trace claims, accepted decisions, procedures, skills, answer views and open runs that depend on it. Agents can classify impact, update derived content, re-run checks and notify the right owners within granted authority. They may not turn an unapproved proposal into policy. A human or explicitly appointed authority decides the business question; routine mechanics and determinate analysis remain agent work.

## Skills, effects and external agents

Skills are editable, versioned product knowledge, not untracked prompt strings. Pin each run to a skill version and state the task preconditions, exact source scope, permitted effect names and targets, budget ceiling, success tests, expected evidence, and escalation conditions. A test suite covers deterministic mappings, known contradictions, stale inputs, permission boundaries, connector outages and previous accepted cases. Evaluation measures the domain outcome and forbidden effects; a green schema check alone is not evidence that a procedure is useful. Provide a read-only or provider-supplied isolated simulation for what-if checks; label every simulated result as such, do not let it write customer state, and never count simulation as proof that a live connector or action works.

Each skill has a named owner and a draft → evaluated → active → superseded/retired lifecycle. An evaluated version can start new work only after activation authorized by that owner, either directly or through a bounded, recorded standing grant for routine refinements. Slate prepares and evaluates the refinement, checks the exact version against that grant, and logs the evaluation, activation and owner authority without another human touch. It cannot self-grant, widen effects, sources, audiences, conditions or budgets, change policy, activate a failed evaluation, or invent authority. A genuinely missing business or scope decision goes to the owner; a routine version decision already delegated does not.

Superseded and retired versions retain immutable pinned history but cannot start new runs. Changing or retiring a version suspends queued and paused runs pinned to it; active runs recheck grants at every boundary and suspend by default on retirement/change. Cancellation, continuation on the exact pinned version, or a successor may follow direct owner authorization or an applicable standing owner grant, with the exact authority recorded. Independently recheck current sources, evidence and live underlying grants before continuation or successor work; a version grant cannot replace those checks. Stop and revocation override any continuation authority. Never silently substitute a version: a successor is a linked new run retaining completed effects and receipts, with ambiguous effects reconciled before further work rather than repeated. Missing, widened, expired or revoked authority leaves the affected decision pending or refused, never implicitly approved.

The proposed lifecycle is Queued → Running → Needs review or Paused → Running, then Completed, Failed or Cancelled. Paused records a resumable reason (such as changed source, unavailable connection or an owner decision); errors produce Failed, not an indefinite pause. Completed, Failed and Cancelled remain terminal: a new linked run, never resurrection, is needed to continue terminal work. Each execution stage retains the current ten-minute Running limit; exceeding it fails the run visibly. A scheduled wait or deliberate pause is durable and releases execution rather than extending a running stage indefinitely. Proposal-only work completes when a member accepts or discards the proposal, recording rejection as today. Granted work completes only when all required effects and output checks are confirmed; request-created or ambiguous effects remain visibly pending and cannot count as completed activation.

Persist each completed stage and its receipts. Local proposal mutations and activity commit atomically, with no partial proposal or product records from a failed/cancelled stage. A later failure or cancellation cannot roll back an earlier confirmed external effect: retain its receipt and completed-stage state, cancel unsent future work, and reconcile in-flight ambiguity. Any compensation is a separately recorded operation requiring a valid grant, never an asserted cross-provider rollback. Show the failure reason, completed effects and remaining uncertainty, and keep manual actions available. Invalid schema or unusable output fails visibly without semantic retries or fallbacks; preserve AI-009's transient-transport-only retry bounds (three attempts over five minutes). External mutations first record durable intent and an idempotency key. On timeout or lost response, reconcile with the provider using that key or read back the resource; do not submit again while the outcome is ambiguous. Show success only after a provider receipt or verified state transition. An unsupported tool fails visibly rather than pretending the step worked.

Expose the same authorized read, citation, proposal, granted execution, status, pause/resume and outcome semantics over the Slate UI, API and MCP. External agents can pin a skill revision and receive a notice when it changes. Exported `SKILL.md` and attachments are snapshots; label them as such and do not imply live synchronization. The app's browser actions use the same server-side grant checks and effect receipts as an agent; generated interfaces cannot bypass them.

Build task-specific interfaces from durable records, skill schemas, and grants. Keep interface definitions portable and source-controlled or exportable, with generated views pointing back to their shared records. A model or provider can be replaced without losing sources, data, skills, decision history, runs, or interface definitions. Complete workspace export and restore must preserve revisions, authority, grants, procedures, receipts and lineage; secrets are reconfigured, and unsupported external state is reported rather than invented.

## Delivery order and lanes

Advance by complete user outcomes. These stages state dependency order, not calendar estimates. Independent work within a stage can proceed in parallel after its shared contract is agreed.

1. **Product and contract baseline.** Map accepted Slate pages/screens and existing clauses to the complete [six proposed user journeys](./USER-JOURNEYS.md), especially the three release workflows: customer SSO inquiry/action/article repair, policy rollout, and research-to-decision/onboarding. Design contextual views around real user tasks while preserving the accepted collaborative workspace and eight policies. Amend the contract and acceptance cases only after review of exact behavior. In parallel, inventory source-owner APIs, permission models and supported effects. Output: reviewed product contract, field definitions, boundary table and executable acceptance fixtures sourced from real permitted exports/accounts.
2. **Durable memory and imports.** Implement record identity, immutable source revisions and passages, claims, scope and authority links, decision supersession, content-free deletion, and extension of the current schema. Complete real Notion/Confluence imports with loss reports and persistent rollback; retain named restrictions and content fidelity. In parallel, build reusable source-evaluation data for known contradictions and scope cases. Gate: an imported workspace can answer “what does this exact revision say, and who approved it?” without inventing authority.
3. **Federated read and collaboration.** Add selected real connectors whose identity, ACL, freshness, lag, deletion and revocation semantics are documented and tested. Keep page editing, comments, inline suggestions and revision history useful while discussion forms decisions. Add source freshness and missing-access states to Ask and Today. In parallel, implement connector-specific evaluation and UI adaptation. Gate: read scopes survive tests for different users, private sources, revocation, deletion, partial index and outage.
4. **Skills and granted action.** Add versioned skills, source-bound context, per-agent identity/grants, durable runs and effect reconciliation. Northstar's paid SSO account feature is distinct from Slate sign-in under `WORK-015`: prove inquiry without effect, then separately authorized purchase/enablement through a real operational integration with provider confirmation/read-back. Use HappyToHelp for its conversation boundary, not a Slate reply substitute. In parallel, add contextual task views, API/MCP action semantics, per-run budget accounting and skill evaluation. Gate: no duplicate effect after an interrupted response, no unapproved policy decision, and confirmed state linked to its current account source.
5. **Proactive maintenance and cross-workflows.** Detect source changes, trace dependencies, show diffs, route true decisions to their authority, update granted derivatives, and revalidate dependent answers and skills. Add policy-change rollout and research-to-decision/onboarding as full journeys. In parallel, test owner-generated interface preservation and source/model replacement. Gate: change propagation is reviewable, all affected outputs reflect the new revision, and unaffected scoped exceptions remain intact.
6. **Useful self-hosted release.** Complete exports and restore, accessibility and load checks, setup/deploy descriptions, connector health reporting, monitoring, account data transitions, and the entire revised contract. Run the same workflows against a clean installation, a customized owner copy, and a restore; verify no proprietary hosted account is needed for an owner's data and source. Gate: every release obligation has direct evidence, and the workflows reduce total effort without lowering their accepted quality floor.

## Sustained-inquiry extension lane

[The frontier extension](./FRONTIER-EXTENSION.md) adds a complete open-question → investigation → bounded experiment → outcome → reusable-learning loop. After stages 2–4 establish real memory, access and run foundations, implement one customer-activation question family alongside stage 5. Preserve all three minimum-release workflows, the full flagship and all 220 current product clauses and 22 baseline obligations; add this loop to the extended flagship acceptance before claiming sustained inquiry. Broaden ambient discovery only after repeated loop outcomes justify it. Any new records or effects require explicit contract and acceptance amendments first.

Acceptance must show a falsified initial hypothesis, useful interim finding, reproducible analysis, predeclared comparison/window/guardrails, authorized effects with exact receipts, and benefit/null/adverse/inconclusive outcome handling that revises a reusable method. Compare against an expert with current tools and a capable generic agent with identical accessible sources/tools; prevent future-outcome leakage in historical cases. Measure discovery quality, missed-important-event recall, reproducibility, forecasts/interventions and all-attempt cost/time/owner attention. Shadow-compare fast routing with direct frontier investigation, including low-score audits and repeated-work savings. Existing privacy, publication, consent, credential, source and run-lifecycle acceptance remains mandatory.

## Minimum useful release and full flagship gate

The minimum useful release is a complete but deliberately bounded operational-memory product, not an SSO-only pilot. It must support collaborative authoring/review in Slate; real incumbent imports; at least one configured document source, one collaboration/evidence source, and one live operational/action system with declared auth and freshness; scoped claim and decision records; and complete versions of three workflows: the Northstar customer SSO answer/action/article-repair loop, a cross-source policy-change impact/rollout, and research-to-decision followed by an onboarding procedure. Customer SSO is not Slate's `WORK-015` sign-in feature. Every workflow must show exact evidence, authority, live-state freshness, grants, decisions, resulting changes and receipts. No source or action may be simulated as a successful production capability.

The full flagship additionally covers multiple real connector families; per-user permission mapping where the provider supports it; partial-index and revocation behavior; user-created skills with evaluations and update notices; generated task interfaces; built-in and external agents with common scopes and receipts; live co-editing; bounded formulas and rollups; user-level restore and owner customization; and proactive impact/repair over the product's complete accepted source graph. A limitation in any selected provider stays explicit in the edition support matrix.

## Acceptance journeys and measures

The source contains core-wiki and flagship implementation with bounded local tests, including 191 explicitly planned contract tests. Those tests do not establish full end-to-end product, deployment, live connector or prospective-value acceptance. Use [HANDOVER.md](./HANDOVER.md) for current verification limits and [ACCEPTANCE.md](./ACCEPTANCE.md) for the required evidence. Each candidate needs freshly executed checks and current provider observations.

Release evidence must include the following real semantic journeys:

- **Incumbent migration:** import actual Notion and Confluence exports and permitted Word/Google Docs documents; compare every supported object and access restriction; report unsupported records; retry, roll back, reload, and restore without silent data loss.
- **Contradiction and applicability:** the Northstar Business SSO rule, obsolete Enterprise article, shipped feature, disabled account, and one-customer discount produce a correct account-scoped answer. Change each source and effective date and verify only applicable claims change.
- **Inquiry and authorized activation:** an eligibility/cost/setup inquiry returns the recorded price/interval, account state and source-backed steps with responsible actors, with no account or charge effect even if a tool grant exists. Missing price or discount expiry stays unknown. Separately, documented account-authorized consent, verified purchase/paid entitlement, applicable commercial approval and an exact operation grant permit one activation with read-back and receipt. Missing any prerequisite prevents activation. Distinguish created request, customer setup pending and confirmed enabled state. Independently granted article repair can succeed in either branch; absent its grant, return a patch. Wrong account, revoked/expired grant, unsupported action or changed policy causes no unauthorized effect.
- **Requester and audience:** use a service credential broader than the asking member, parallel investigators with different scopes, persisted task views and external-agent outputs; prove no restricted content or indirect hints reach the narrower reader. Change the background audience/destination or a source/user grant before fan-in, write, delivery and later read; reauthorize all contributors and withhold unauthorized artifacts, including public-page copies.
- **Freshness, revocation and deletion:** interrupt connector sync; show lag/coverage; revoke a principal and verify source passages, answers, proposals, citations, and downstream runs cannot reveal its content; delete a source and preserve only allowed tombstones.
- **Change and repair:** revise a policy, inspect the dependent-object diff, approve the true business decision, update a procedure within grant, re-run its evaluation, invalidate old answers and confirm a scoped exception remains scoped.
- **Credential lifecycle:** issue a scoped connector/external-agent credential, revoke or disconnect it with queued, paused and in-flight work, and verify immediate dispatch blocking and visible unavailable state. Reconnect with a different identity or narrower scope; prove resynchronization and authorization precede retained-content access and resume, and unresolved external effects are reconciled only with valid authority.
- **Run lifecycle and atomicity:** exercise ten-minute stage timeout, provider/budget/schema failure and cancellation before a local proposal commit; verify no partial proposal/product change and readable error with manual actions available. Fail or cancel a later stage after a confirmed remote effect: retain the receipt, cancel unsent work, reconcile pending effects and require a separate valid compensation grant. Terminal runs cannot resume; deliberate non-terminal pause can resume after reauthorization. Invalid output is not automatically retried or replaced with a fallback.
- **Interrupted effect:** cut the connection after an external write may have occurred. Resume and reconcile the provider receipt/state without duplicate creation or a false success.
- **Skill regression:** pin a skill version, add a contradiction or adverse case, show the old evaluation, test a candidate revision, and notify external agents of the update. Failed evaluations do not become active. Prove an evaluated routine refinement activates under its exact standing owner grant, and applicable continuation or a linked successor proceeds without a new human touch. Missing, widened, expired or revoked grants keep the decision pending or refused; real business/scope choices still require their owner. Supersede/retire an owned skill with new, queued, paused and active runs: block new runs from old versions, suspend pinned work by default, and preserve immutable history. Independently check current sources and live grants; recorded finish/successor authority never overrides stop or revocation, changes policy or scope, or silently substitutes a version. A successor retains completed effects and receipts and reconciles ambiguity before further work without duplication.
- **Portability and ownership:** export, restore and reproduce decisions, citations, grants, skill history, open/closed runs and owner-customized interfaces; swap the configured model/provider and keep non-AI operations and durable data usable.

Use zero tolerance for unauthorized reads or effects, lost/duplicated external mutations, citations that silently resolve to a different revision, false policy approval, and revoked content disclosure. For supported claims, 100% of surfaced answers/actions must identify their source revision, applicable scope, authority state and freshness; unsupported or stale sources must be visibly marked rather than represented as current. Measure task correctness against pre-registered expected outcomes and real source state. For economic value, compare the same tasks with the manual incumbent path and the uncustomized/owner-customized Slate path: count every attempt, model/tool cost, elapsed time, source and deployment work, expert interruption, owner review, failed run, and later repair. The outcome must preserve the agreed quality floor while reducing total work and owner attention; a cheap failed run does not count as a saving.

The proposed attention target is not a current result: assume the relevant sources are connected and existing standing grants cover the requested work. Then a first useful result needs no migration, schema, agent or model setup; each genuinely missing source permission or consequential decision is asked once to its named owner; and routine work requires no progress watching or repeated approval. A provider may still require its owner to grant credentials. Acceptance counts every requestor, administrator, source owner, expert and colleague touch—including credentials, deployment, migration, preparation, interruptions and later repair—so the system cannot claim a saving by transferring hidden work to someone else.

## Reuse and custody

Slate remains the product and integration owner. Keep its code and contract in this repository; do not regenerate it from Base, Delta, a family or a support edition. The maintained Base foundation is an optional technical starting point, not an instruction to replace Slate's existing source or accepted UI.

Evaluate specific reusable source by its actual APIs, owner, dependencies and tests. HappyToHelp's `src/modules/knowledge/` offers corpus staging, retained versions, citations, provider import adapters, publication and the invariant that failed refresh leaves the last published corpus intact; inspect the documented source-array materialization limit before choosing it. `src/modules/ai-support/` supplies evidence packet and schema-validation patterns; `src/modules/customer-context/` supplies source-revision checks before publishing work. Their H2H identity, schema and support-resolution policy remain H2H-owned. Orbit's `src/core/features/signals/`, `opportunities/`, and `outreach/` carry event attribution, stale recommendation/draft checks and uncertain message reconciliation; reuse those patterns or source only through a concrete selectable boundary. Delta's exact-passage and evidence classification can inform Slate but does not move Delta's purpose or permissions. Any shared implementation needs a real Slate adoption and requirement-specific verification before it is described as shared.

Integrations must preserve each external service's independent deployment, identity and authority boundaries.
